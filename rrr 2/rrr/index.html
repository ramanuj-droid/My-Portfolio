AI- Powered Travel Discovery Website

A fully interactive travel discovery website that enables users to explore destinations, plan trips and get inspired using AI-style itinerary logic and real-time data. Spreadsheet for Business Decision

Spreadsheet dashboards for business decision - making

A complete business analytics project focused on customer retention and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) using an online retail dataset. Through spreadsheet analytics, I segmented customers into monthly cohorts, calculated CLTV for every cohort, and built a retention matrix showing how customer repeat-purchase behaviour evolves over time. Customer retention was visualised through percentage tables and graphs to reveal how cohorts expand, contract and stabilise, enabling clear interpretation of customer behaviour patterns. The final dashboard, powered by pivot tables and step-by-step transformations, offers leaders insight into customer value, retention performance and revenue growth opportunities.
